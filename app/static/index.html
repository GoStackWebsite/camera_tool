Staff detection

Learned uniform model
Not installed: the colour rules below are used. Train one from labelled captures with tools\staff_train.py (or the MCP tool train_staff_model); the file models\staff_cls.pt can be copied to every store of the chain.
Uniform colours
Staff are recognised by a staff zone (draw one behind the counter), by uniform colour (open a camera and press "Staff" on a person wearing the uniform), or by long presence. Staff are excluded from footfall and enable the unattended-customer and phone checks.

Demographics (anonymous age band & gender)

Works best on an entrance camera at face height (faces ≥ 40 px). No face images are stored, only the age band and gender of each visit.
